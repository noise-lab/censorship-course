| 6 | **Lever 2: Make the Platform Open the Box** | • **Read first:** [European Commission, preliminary findings on TikTok and Meta (Oct 24, 2025)](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-preliminarily-finds-tiktok-and-meta-breach-their-transparency-obligations-under-digital); [Commission roundtable on vetted-researcher access (May 19, 2026)](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-holds-roundtable-data-access-vetted-researchers).<br>• Delegated act (Reg. 2025/2050) in force Oct 29, 2025; 49 applications at the May 2026 roundtable.<br>• Say "first requests in the pipeline"; no public record found yet of data delivered.<br>• Ask: does this help a US researcher studying a US election? |
| 7 | **Lever 3: Make the Platform Report** | • **Read first:** [Santa Clara Principles](https://santaclaraprinciples.org/) (2.0, Dec 8, 2021); [CDT summary of 2.0](https://cdt.org/insights/santa-clara-principles-2-0-civil-society-recommendations-for-how-companies-states-should-protect-free-expression-rights-online/).<br>• DSA Transparency Database: 9B+ statements in H1 2025, ~99% on platforms' own terms (figure from the measurement lecture).<br>• **Correction, label it:** the legal lecture's "What the DSA Still Misses" slide lists demonetization and downranking. | 8 | **Lever 4: Appeal the Decision** | • **Read first:** [Appeals Centre Europe, second transparency report (May 28, 2026)](https://www.appealscentre.eu/transparency-report-shows-big-increase-in-users-challenging-social-media-decisions/); [first report (Oct 1, 2025)](https://www.appealscentre.eu/appeals-centre-publishes-first-transparency-report/).<br>• 24,000+ disputes Apr 2025–Mar 2026; 59% disagreement where reviewed; 7,300+ default decisions; 70% overturn on hate speech left up.<br>• Appeal runs both ways: it also orders removals.<br>• Limit: Art. 21 decisions are not binding. |
| 9 | **Where Appeal Can't Reach** | • Callback to the GIFCT slide in the platform lecture (2025 report, May 21, 2026, ~408,000 hashes).<br>• No poster notice, no appeal, a wrong hash is wrong everywhere.<br>• Discussion: who audits a list nobody may see? Prompt toward trusted third parties or sampled review. |
| 10 | **Lever 5: Leave** | • **Read first:** [Bluesky (Wikipedia, figures to Oct 2026)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bluesky); [Engadget on WhatsApp third-party chats](https://www.engadget.com/apps/whatsapp-enables-interoperability-with-two-other-messengers-in-the-eu-140000835.html).<br>• Mastodon ~10.5M accounts, ~785k MAU (joinmastodon stats, 2026). Bluesky 47M registered; ~10M mobile MAU is a Similarweb estimate, not official.<br>• Limits: network effects; moderation moves to server admins and labelers; a federated server is still a host.<br>• Ask who has moved platforms and whether their audience followed. |
| 11 | **The Chill Is an Asymmetry** | • Callback to the notice-and-takedown slide in the legal lecture.<br>• Counter-notice: sworn statement, 10–14 business days, consent to jurisdiction (17 U.S.C. §512(g)).<br>• Ask: would you sign to get a critical review back up? |
| 12 | **Contesting a Takedown: youtube-dl** | • **Read first:** [TechCrunch, Nov 16, 2020](https://techcrunch.com/2020/11/16/github-defies-riaa-takedown-notice-restoring-youtube-dl-and-starting-1m-defense-fund); [EFF letter in GitHub's DMCA repo](https://github.com/github/dmca/blob/master/2020/11/2020-11-16-RIAA-reversal-effletter.pdf).<br>• The class guessed wrong about who sent the notice; use it.<br>• Worked because of fame, lawyers, and a willing host.
